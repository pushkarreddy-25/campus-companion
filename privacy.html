What the app collects

Campus Companion collects the email address used for an account, your name, branch, semester, attendance records, timetable entries, and uploaded PDF resources.

How data is stored and used

Account and app data are stored in Supabase. The data is used only to run Campus Companion, including account access, attendance tracking, timetable display, and the academic library. A copy of app data is also stored locally in your browser so the app can work offline.

The app loads no third-party fonts, analytics, ads, or trackers. The only external service is Supabase, which provides authentication, database, and file storage.

Who can see the data

Your attendance records are private to you. Approved library files are visible to logged-in users. Your profile and account data are used to provide your account and are not published as library content.

Deleting data

Use the Reset button in your profile to remove locally stored profile, subject, timetable, and attendance data. To request deletion of account data stored in Supabase, email pushkarkarri767@gmail.com. You can also contact pushkarkarri767@gmail.com about uploaded PDFs or other data deletion requests.
